Expectant AI

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 • Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Expectant AI ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an AI-powered pregnancy companion designed to help expectant parents organize prenatal health records, track daily wellness and physical activity, and receive supportive, stage-aware insights. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use and protect it, and how we handle data accessed through Google APIs.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo URL) to authenticate and identify your account.
  • Pregnancy & Health Profile Data: Information you voluntarily enter, including expected due date, last menstrual period (LMP), maternal date of birth, blood group, care provider details, vital measurements, medications, appointments, reminders, daily journal entries, and uploaded medical reports.
  • Google Health Physical Activity Data (Optional): When you explicitly click Sync Google Health and grant permission to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly scope, Expectant AI reads your past 7 days of physical activity metrics (daily step counts, active minutes, distance, active calories burned, and exercise sessions).
  • Google Drive App-Created Files (Optional): When you upload medical reports or chat attachments and grant permission to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, Expectant AI stores and retrieves only the specific files created or uploaded by you through Expectant AI inside your dedicated "expectant-ai" Google Drive folder.
  • Google Calendar Data (Optional): When you explicitly sync prenatal appointments or reminders to Google Calendar and grant permission to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created scope, Expectant AI creates and manages a dedicated secondary calendar named "Expectant AI" on your Google Calendar account to schedule your prenatal checkups and daily reminders.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your pregnancy timeline, health measurements, appointments, and 7-day physical activity progress in your private dashboard.
  • To generate personalized, stage-specific prenatal wellness cards, daily digests, report summaries, and conversational answers through the MIRA assistant using Google Gemini AI.
  • To create and update appointment and reminder events inside your dedicated "Expectant AI" Google Calendar when requested by you.
Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure):

Expectant AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only access Google Health and Google Calendar data after your explicit consent.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your Google user data for serving advertisements or training generalized foundation AI models.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your profile, daily logs, and synced activity summaries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore scoped strictly to your authenticated user account (and any family member you explicitly invite via profile sharing). All communications between your browser, Firebase, Google APIs, and the Google Gemini API are encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS.

4. Revoking Access & Deleting Your Data

  • You can revoke Expectant AI's access to your Google account, Google Health, or Google Calendar at any time from your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.
  • You can delete individual health logs, measurements, reports, appointments, or your entire pregnancy profile directly within the application.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the developer via the project repository at github.com/panchalshubham0608/expectant-ai.
